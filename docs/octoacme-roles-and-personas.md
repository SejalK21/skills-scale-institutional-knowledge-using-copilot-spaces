# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Core Personas

### Developers

#### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

#### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

#### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

#### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

#### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partner with **Technical Lead** on architecture and design decisions
- Report progress and blockers to **Project Manager** and **Scrum Master**
- Collaborate with **QA Lead** on test coverage and quality criteria
- Engage with **Business Analyst** for requirements clarity

---

### Product Managers

#### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

#### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

#### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

#### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

#### Interaction with Other Roles
- Work with **Business Analyst** to gather and refine requirements
- Collaborate with **Design/UX Researcher** on user needs and usability
- Align with **Project Manager** on timelines and resource planning
- Consult with **Technical Lead** on feasibility and trade-offs

---

### Project Managers

#### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

#### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

#### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

#### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

#### Interaction with Other Roles
- Work with **Scrum Master** to facilitate ceremonies and remove impediments
- Escalate risks and dependencies identified by **Technical Lead** and **QA Lead**
- Coordinate **Release Manager** activities for deployment readiness
- Partner with **Product Manager** on scope and priority alignment

---

## Specialized Roles

### Technical Lead / Architect

#### Role Summary
Technical Leads define technical strategy, make architectural decisions, and ensure code quality and system scalability. They serve as the primary technical advisor to Product Managers and Project Managers, enabling teams to deliver scalable and maintainable solutions.

#### Responsibilities
- Define technical direction and architectural approach for projects
- Review design proposals and code architecture
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Mentor developers and guide technical problem-solving
- Ensure alignment with platform standards and best practices
- Support capacity planning and technology selection
- Collaborate with QA on testability and performance requirements

#### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable technical solutions
- Minimize technical debt while shipping incrementally
- Enable team velocity through good architecture and tooling
- Reduce production incidents through robust design

#### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review comments and technical guidance
- Planning conversations about technical feasibility
- Risk identification and mitigation discussions

#### Interaction with Other Roles
- Guide **Developers** on technical decisions and best practices
- Advise **Product Managers** on technical feasibility and trade-offs
- Work with **QA Lead** to ensure testability and performance standards
- Support **Project Manager** in identifying technical risks and dependencies

---

### QA Lead

#### Role Summary
QA Leads own quality strategy, define testing approaches, and ensure acceptance criteria are met. They lead the QA team and work closely with Product and Development to ensure quality gates are effective and production readiness is achieved.

#### Responsibilities
- Define quality strategy and testing approach for projects
- Create and maintain test plans and acceptance criteria
- Lead QA team members and coordinate testing efforts
- Identify quality risks and propose mitigation
- Report on quality metrics and test coverage
- Collaborate with Product on acceptance criteria clarity
- Establish quality gates and release readiness criteria

#### Goals
- Ensure features meet acceptance criteria before release
- Reduce production defects through comprehensive testing
- Enable fast feedback cycles with efficient test automation
- Maintain high quality standards and customer satisfaction

#### Typical Communication
- QA planning sessions and test strategy discussions
- Defect triage and prioritization meetings
- Quality reports and metrics reviews
- Release readiness assessments

#### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partner with **Developers** on test coverage and automation
- Collaborate with **Technical Lead** on testability and performance requirements
- Work with **Product Manager** to validate acceptance criteria
- Support **Release Manager** with quality sign-off for deployments

---

### Scrum Master / Agile Coach

#### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies, remove team impediments, and help teams continuously improve their processes. They serve as process coaches rather than task managers, fostering psychological safety and enabling team effectiveness.

#### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Remove impediments blocking team progress
- Coach team on agile practices and continuous improvement
- Track team velocity and process metrics
- Escalate organizational barriers to improvement
- Support process adaptation based on team feedback
- Foster a culture of psychological safety and continuous learning

#### Goals
- Maximize team velocity and predictability
- Create psychological safety for feedback and learning
- Institutionalize continuous improvement culture
- Enable team autonomy and self-organization

#### Typical Communication
- Facilitation of all agile ceremonies
- One-on-ones with team members
- Retrospective action item follow-up
- Impediment escalation to Project Manager

#### Interaction with Other Roles
- Support **Project Manager** in coordination and communication
- Coach all team members on agile practices
- Facilitate **Retrospectives** to drive continuous improvement
- Help **Developers** resolve team-level blockers

---

### Business Analyst

#### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge business needs and technical solutions. They gather requirements, clarify acceptance criteria, and ensure solutions align with business objectives. They are key to reducing rework and accelerating delivery through early clarity.

#### Responsibilities
- Conduct requirements gathering with stakeholders
- Clarify and document acceptance criteria
- Identify business constraints and dependencies
- Validate that solutions meet business needs
- Support product managers with requirements analysis
- Document business rules and edge cases
- Facilitate communication between business and technical teams

#### Goals
- Ensure solutions solve real business problems
- Reduce rework through clear requirements
- Accelerate delivery through early clarity
- Minimize misalignment between business intent and technical delivery

#### Typical Communication
- Requirements workshops and stakeholder interviews
- Acceptance criteria review with development teams
- Business constraint documentation and communication
- Clarification of ambiguous requirements

#### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partner with **Product Manager** to refine and validate requirements
- Work with **Developers** to clarify acceptance criteria and edge cases
- Collaborate with **Technical Lead** on feasibility and technical constraints
- Support **QA Lead** in defining comprehensive test scenarios

---

### Design / UX Researcher

#### Role Summary
Design and UX Researchers define user experience requirements, conduct user research, and validate design decisions. They represent the user voice in product decisions, ensuring solutions are both usable and delightful.

#### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Define UX requirements and design specifications
- Validate design decisions with user feedback
- Collaborate with product on feature prioritization
- Ensure accessibility and usability standards are met
- Create design documentation and patterns
- Advocate for user needs throughout the development process

#### Goals
- Deliver user-centric solutions that delight customers
- Reduce feature rework through validated design
- Maintain consistent user experience across products
- Ensure solutions are accessible to all users

#### Typical Communication
- Design critique sessions and user testing sessions
- Requirements refinement conversations
- UX documentation and pattern library maintenance
- User research findings and recommendations

#### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partner with **Product Manager** on feature prioritization and user needs
- Collaborate with **Developers** on implementation feasibility
- Work with **Business Analyst** to ensure user needs align with business goals
- Support **QA Lead** in defining usability test scenarios

---

### Release Manager

#### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate deployment schedules, manage release documentation, and ensure smooth production deployments. They own the release readiness process and minimize risk through careful coordination and communication.

#### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release schedules and deployment windows
- Prepare release notes and deployment documentation
- Ensure rollback plans are documented and tested
- Manage release communication to stakeholders
- Track deployment readiness across teams
- Coordinate post-deployment verification
- Manage dependencies and coordination with infrastructure teams

#### Goals
- Minimize deployment risk and downtime
- Enable predictable, reliable releases
- Maintain clear communication throughout release cycle
- Ensure rapid recovery from deployment issues

#### Typical Communication
- Release planning and coordination meetings
- Release notes and stakeholder communication
- Post-deployment verification and retrospectives
- Incident communication and rollback coordination

#### Interaction with Other Roles
- Coordinate with **Project Manager** on release timing and readiness
- Work with **QA Lead** to confirm quality sign-off before deployment
- Ensure **Technical Lead** has validated architecture and configurations
- Communicate with **Developers** on deployment procedures and rollback steps

---

### Security / Compliance Officer

#### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Officers ensure projects meet security and regulatory requirements. They review security implications and manage security-related risks, integrating security into the development process from inception.

#### Responsibilities
- Review projects for security implications
- Ensure compliance with regulatory and internal standards
- Identify security risks and propose mitigations
- Conduct security reviews and threat modeling
- Track security metrics and vulnerability management
- Provide security guidance and training
- Manage security incident response and reporting

#### Goals
- Prevent security incidents and data breaches
- Maintain compliance with regulatory requirements
- Build security into development processes
- Enable teams to ship secure solutions confidently

#### Typical Communication
- Security review sessions and threat modeling
- Compliance audit participation
- Security incident response and escalation
- Security training and guidance sessions

#### Interaction with Other Roles
- Review projects with **Product Manager** and **Technical Lead** for security implications
- Collaborate with **Developers** on secure coding practices
- Support **Project Manager** in identifying and tracking security risks
- Advise **Release Manager** on security readiness for deployments

- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance
